AI Forward Deployed Engineer

A forward deployed engineer sits with a client and ships AI that changes how their business runs. In this course you play that role for Wayfarer, a fictional travel insurer: you build an agent that decides claims, prove it with evaluations, make it durable, safe and able to improve itself, deploy it to production, and hand it over.

The client

Wayfarer sells travel insurance on three plans, Saver, Standard and Elite. Three claims handlers, Rosa Delgado (the senior handler), Ben Achterberg and Ifeoma Nwosu, work a queue by hand: for each claim they read the story and the documents, check them against the policy wording and the claims manual, and approve it with a payout, decline it, or refer it for someone's judgment. Rosa signs off any payout over $1,000.

The head of claims wants the queue handled straight through by an AI agent, as accurately as the handlers, with a person still signing off large payouts. That brief, and a measurable target, is what you'll deliver.

Before you begin

You should be comfortable with Python and with calling an LLM API, and ideally have built a tool-using agent. The Agentic AI course covers the frameworks used here, and Harness Engineering goes deeper on the harness. Week 3 deploys to AWS; every step that costs money has a free route on Render instead, and the course says which is which.

How each module works

  1. Objectives say what you'll be able to do.
  2. The build box lists what you add to the Wayfarer project.
  3. The lessons explain the ideas with short code, and a hands-on simulator lets you try the key idea in the page.
  4. Pitfalls, takeaways, a quiz and an exercise close the module, followed by references.

Much of the product is built with a coding agent working from short written briefs, the way FDEs increasingly work. You stay accountable for what ships: you write the brief, check the plan, and review the result.

Where this syllabus comes from

The structure and the projects follow Ed Donner's undercover repository, which accompanies his course AI FDE: The Forward Deployed Engineer & Agentic AI Architect. There, the client is a fictional insurer called UnderCover. This course teaches the same subjects with its own client, data, code and exercises, so you don't need the repository; each module links to the matching day if you want a reference implementation. Written in November 2026. Model and cloud product names change quickly, so check what's current before you rely on one.
